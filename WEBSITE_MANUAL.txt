================================================================================
  SECTION 2: VISITOR QUICK START
================================================================================

HOW TO BROWSE AND ENROLL IN A COURSE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/
  Step 2: Click "Courses" in the navigation bar, or go to
          https://pacificbarista.com.np/courses
  Step 3: Browse the course cards and click on any course you are
          interested in.
  Step 4: Read the full course details — syllabus, duration, and fees.
  Step 5: Click the "Enroll" button on the course page, or go to
          https://pacificbarista.com.np/enroll
  Step 6: Fill in the enrollment form with your name, email, phone,
          and select your course.
  Step 7: Submit the form. The academy team will contact you to confirm.

HOW TO VERIFY A CERTIFICATE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/verify
          OR scan the QR code printed on the certificate.
  Step 2: Enter the certificate code (e.g., PB-2026-001) in the box.
  Step 3: Click "Verify".
  Step 4: The certificate details will appear on the screen — student name,
          course, issue date, and validity.

HOW TO CONTACT THE ACADEMY:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/contact
  Step 2: Fill in your name, email, and message.
  Step 3: Click "Send Message".
  Step 4: The admin team will reply to you via email.


================================================================================
  SECTION 3: ADMIN LOGIN
================================================================================

Admin Login URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific

HOW TO LOG IN (Step-by-Step):
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific
  Step 2: Enter your admin email address.
  Step 3: Enter your password.
  Step 4: Click the "Send OTP" button.
  Step 5: Open your email inbox and find the email from Pacific Barista
          with a 6-digit OTP code.
  Step 6: Enter the 6-digit OTP on the login page.
  Step 7: Click "Verify & Sign In".
  Step 8: You will be redirected to the Admin Dashboard.

  Note: Your login session lasts 7 days. After 7 days, you will need
  to log in again from Step 1.


================================================================================
  SECTION 4: ADMIN DASHBOARD — ALL FEATURES
================================================================================

After logging in, you land on the Dashboard at:
https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/dashboard

Use the sidebar menu to navigate to any section.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.1 MANAGE COURSES
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/courses
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO ADD A NEW COURSE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/courses
  Step 2: Click the "Add Course" button (top right).
  Step 3: Fill in the course form:
          - Title (e.g., Barista Basics)
          - Slug (auto-generated or custom, e.g., barista-basics)
          - Description
          - Duration (e.g., 2 Weeks)
          - Fee (e.g., NPR 5000)
          - Level (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
          - Syllabus items
          - Thumbnail image (upload from your device)
          - Featured (Yes/No toggle)
  Step 4: Click "Save Course".
  Step 5: The course will now appear on the public Courses page.

HOW TO EDIT A COURSE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/courses
  Step 2: Find the course you want to edit in the list.
  Step 3: Click the pencil/edit icon next to the course.
  Step 4: Update the fields you want to change.
  Step 5: Click "Save Course" to apply changes.

HOW TO DELETE A COURSE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/courses
  Step 2: Click the trash/delete icon next to the course.
  Step 3: Confirm the deletion in the popup.
  Note: Deleted courses cannot be recovered.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.2 MANAGE BLOG POSTS
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/blogs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO ADD A NEW BLOG POST:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/blogs
  Step 2: Click "Add Blog" button.
  Step 3: Fill in the blog form:
          - Title
          - Slug (e.g., latte-art-tips)
          - Content (rich text / full article body)
          - Excerpt (short summary shown on blog listing page)
          - Author
          - Category
          - Tags
          - Thumbnail / Cover image (upload from your device)
          - Published (Yes/No toggle — unpublished blogs are not visible
            to the public)
  Step 4: Click "Save Blog".
  Step 5: The blog post will now appear on the public Blog page
          (if Published is set to Yes).

HOW TO EDIT A BLOG POST:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/blogs
  Step 2: Click the edit icon next to the blog post.
  Step 3: Make your changes.
  Step 4: Click "Save Blog".

HOW TO DELETE A BLOG POST:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/blogs
  Step 2: Click the delete icon next to the post.
  Step 3: Confirm deletion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.3 MANAGE TRAINERS
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/trainers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO ADD A NEW TRAINER:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/trainers
  Step 2: Click the "Add Trainer" button.
  Step 3: Fill in the trainer form:
          - Full Name (e.g., Rajesh Shrestha)
          - Designation / Title (e.g., Head Barista Trainer)
          - Bio / About (short description of the trainer)
          - Specialization (e.g., Latte Art, Espresso, Brewing)
          - Experience (e.g., 5 Years)
          - Profile Photo (upload from your device)
          - Social Media Links (optional — Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn)
          - Display Order (number to control the order they appear on
            the public Trainers page)
  Step 4: Click "Save Trainer".
  Step 5: The trainer will now appear on the public Trainers page.

HOW TO EDIT A TRAINER:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/trainers
  Step 2: Click the edit icon next to the trainer.
  Step 3: Update the fields as needed.
  Step 4: Click "Save Trainer".

HOW TO DELETE A TRAINER:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/trainers
  Step 2: Click the delete icon next to the trainer.
  Step 3: Confirm deletion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.4 MANAGE TESTIMONIALS
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/testimonials

HOW TO ADD A TESTIMONIAL:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/testimonials

HOW TO EDIT OR DELETE A TESTIMONIAL:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.6 MANAGE ENROLLMENTS
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/enrollments
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO VIEW ENROLLMENTS:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/enrollments
  Step 2: You will see a table of all enrollment submissions with
          student name, email, phone, course, and submission date.
  Step 3: Click on any row to see full enrollment details.

HOW TO UPDATE ENROLLMENT STATUS:
  Step 1: Open the enrollment you want to update.
  Step 2: Change the status (e.g., Pending → Confirmed → Completed).
  Step 3: Save the change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.7 MANAGE CERTIFICATES
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/certificates
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO ISSUE A NEW CERTIFICATE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/certificates
  Step 2: Click "Issue Certificate".
  Step 3: Fill in the form:
          - Student Name
          - Course Name
          - Issue Date
          - Expiry Date (if applicable)
          - Certificate Code (auto-generated or custom,
            e.g., PB-2026-001)
  Step 4: Click "Issue".
  Step 5: The system generates a unique QR code for this certificate.
  Step 6: Print or share the certificate with the student.
  Step 7: The student can verify it at:
          https://pacificbarista.com.np/verify

HOW TO VIEW OR DELETE A CERTIFICATE:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/certificates
  Step 2: Search by certificate code or student name.
  Step 3: Click to view full details, or click delete to remove it.

  4.8 MANAGE CONTACTS
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/contacts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO VIEW CONTACT MESSAGES:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/contacts
  Step 2: You will see all messages submitted through the public
          Contact page (/contact).
  Step 3: Click on any message to read the full details (name, email,

HOW TO DELETE A CONTACT MESSAGE:
  Step 1: Find the message in the list.

  Step 3: Confirm deletion.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.9 MANAGE ADMINS (Super Admin Only)
  URL: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/admins
  Note: Only the Super Admin can access this section.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO ADD A NEW ADMIN:
  Step 1: Log in as Super Admin.
  Step 2: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/admins
  Step 3: Click "Add Admin".
  Step 4: Fill in the form:
          - Full Name
          - Email Address (this will be their login email)
          - Password (set a strong password for them)
          - Role (Admin or Super Admin)
  Step 5: Click "Create Admin".
  Step 6: Share the email and password with the new admin.
          They can log in at: https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific

HOW TO EDIT AN ADMIN:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/admins
  Step 2: Click the edit icon next to the admin you want to update.
  Step 3: Change their name, email, password (leave blank to keep
          existing password), or role.
  Step 4: Click "Update Admin".

HOW TO DELETE AN ADMIN:
  Step 1: Go to https://pacificbarista.com.np/admin-pacific/admins
  Step 2: Click the delete icon next to the admin.
  Step 3: Confirm deletion.
  Note: You cannot delete your own Super Admin account.

================================================================================
  SECTION 6: TECHNICAL INFORMATION
================================================================================

Frontend:   React + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4
Backend:    Node.js + Express.js + MongoDB (Mongoose)
Database:   MongoDB Atlas (cloud-hosted)
Hosting:    Frontend → Vercel | Backend → Render
Email:      Resend API (used for OTP delivery)
Storage:    Cloudinary (used for all image uploads)
Domain:     pacificbarista.com.np

API Base URL (Production):
  https://pacific-barista-api.onrender.com/api
  (or as configured in your .env file)
